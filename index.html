For My Special Person

Tum meri zindagi ka woh khoobsurat hissa ho, jiske saath har chhoti khushi bhi khaas ban jaati hai. Is Karwa Chauth par meri dua hai ki hamare beech pyaar, bharosa aur muskurahat hamesha bani rahe.

Jaise chaand raat ko roshan karta hai, waise hi tum meri duniya mein khushiyan bhar dete ho. Har din tumhara saath mile aur har kal aaj se zyada khoobsurat ho. 💕

Meri Dil Se Dua 💌

Tumhari har khwahish poori ho, tumhare chehre ki muskurahat kabhi kam na ho, aur zindagi ke har mod par tumhein pyaar, sukoon aur khushiyan milti rahein.

Happy Karwa Chauth, my love! ❤️
